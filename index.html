CORE

What Is Social Arbitrage?

Exploiting the gap between what the real world knows and what Wall Street has priced in. Institutions miss what's happening on TikTok, in malls, in school playgrounds. That gap is your edge.

"Wall Street is focused on the 'what', but as a social arb investor, you focus on the 'why' before the 'what' shows up in the numbers."
RULE 1

Buy the Information Imbalance

Buy when you discover something the market hasn't priced in. Sell when Wall Street catches up. The moment CNBC talks about it, your edge is gone.

"I buy a company's stock upon discovery of an information imbalance... and sell when that information becomes widely accepted as fact by Wall Street."
RULE 2

Zero Financial Literacy Required

Ignore earnings reports, P/E ratios, analyst upgrades. That's backward-looking noise. Your edge is forward-looking social observation — what are people buying and talking about that hasn't shown up in the numbers yet?

"What I do requires zero financial literacy."
RULE 3

No Stop Losses. Ever.

Stop losses kick you out of great trades on noise. What changes your mind is not price action — it's a change in the underlying social signal. Price is not your signal. People are.

"Never ever use stop losses." — Dumb Money Live
RULE 4

Concentrate When Convicted

When you've found a real information imbalance, concentrate. Diversification is what you do when you don't know something. Small positions on high-conviction ideas don't make sense.

"Invest with concentration, not diversification, when you have high conviction."
RULE 5

Sell Into Mainstream

When Bloomberg or CNBC runs your story, that's your exit — not your entry. The imbalance has been arbitraged away. Take the profit and find the next one.

"Sell when the story you discovered hits the mainstream news."
